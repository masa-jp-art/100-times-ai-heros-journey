# ビジュアルプロンプト

## protagonist

Morin Sou is a 28-year-old male architect with a slender, slightly angular build that reflects his introverted nature. His face features sharp, high cheekbones and a strong jawline, framed by messy, medium-length silver-grey hair that falls casually over his forehead, suggesting a lack of concern for conventional grooming. His eyes are a striking, deep steel-blue, conveying an intense, analytical gaze that often appears distant or guarded, hinting at his psychological walls. He wears a minimalist, modern ensemble: a structured charcoal-grey wool blazer over a crisp white turtleneck sweater, paired with dark tailored trousers and polished black leather boots. The fabric textures are matte and high-quality, emphasizing his professional status and preference for order. In his hands, he holds a rolled-up architectural blueprint and a sleek, black digital tablet, symbols of his creative control. The atmosphere is moody and introspective, set against a backdrop of blurred, geometric city lights. Soft, cool-toned ambient lighting highlights the contours of his face and the texture of his clothing, creating a sense of isolation yet sophistication. The overall vibe is one of quiet tension and intellectual elegance, capturing the moment where his rigid self-image begins to crack, revealing a subtle vulnerability beneath his composed exterior.

## messenger

Sakakibara Makoto, a distinguished 60-year-old architect, stands with a slight stoop that speaks of decades spent hunched over drafting tables. His physique is lean and wiry, wrapped in a worn, charcoal-grey wool overcoat that drapes loosely over his frame, suggesting a man who prioritizes function over fashion. His face is deeply lined, bearing the topography of wisdom and quiet contemplation, with deep-set eyes that hold a piercing yet gentle intensity. His hair is silver-white, thinning at the temples but still neatly combed back, reflecting a man who maintains order even in his personal appearance. He wears simple, dark trousers and leather shoes that have seen better days, grounding his ethereal presence in reality. In his hands, he holds a rolled-up blueprint, the paper yellowed with age, symbolizing the unfinished dreams and unspoken connections he carries. The lighting is soft and diffused, filtering through large industrial windows, casting long, subtle shadows that emphasize the texture of his skin and the fabric of his coat. The atmosphere is one of serene melancholy, a quiet space where silence speaks louder than words. His expression is not one of judgment, but of open invitation, as if the very air around him is an open door, waiting for someone to step through. The overall mood is contemplative, inviting the viewer to reflect on the beauty of imperfection and the strength found in shared vulnerability.

## supporter

A mid-40s male ancient architecture restorer with a lean, weathered physique and calm, introspective demeanor. His face features deep-set, gentle eyes, a straight nose, and a faint, knowing smile, framed by salt-and-pepper hair swept back loosely. He wears a simple, earth-toned linen shirt with rolled-up sleeves, revealing forearms dusted with stone powder, paired with worn brown trousers and sturdy leather boots. In his hands, he holds a chipped trowel and a fragment of aged brick, symbolizing his philosophy of embracing imperfection. The scene is bathed in soft, natural sunlight filtering through a dusty workshop window, casting long, gentle shadows that highlight the texture of his clothing and the rough materials around him. The atmosphere is serene and contemplative, evoking a sense of quiet acceptance and timeless wisdom.

## adversary

A sharp, androgynous man in his late twenties with a lean, angular physique and an unnerving stillness. His face is pale and gaunt, featuring high cheekbones, sharp jawline, and piercing, emotionless eyes that lack warmth, conveying cold intellectual superiority. His hair is jet black, cut in a severe, asymmetrical bob that frames his face with geometric precision, emphasizing his minimalist aesthetic. He wears a monochromatic ensemble of charcoal grey and stark white: a tailored, sleeveless long coat with clean, architectural lines and no visible buttons or zippers, paired with slim black trousers and minimalist black boots. The fabric appears stiff and structured, rejecting softness. He holds a sleek, matte-black architectural model of an abstract, void-like structure in one gloved hand, the object reflecting his philosophy of pure, empty space. The atmosphere is sterile and oppressive, dominated by cool blue and grey tones. Lighting is harsh and directional, casting sharp, geometric shadows that accentuate the lines of his clothing and the model, creating a sense of isolation and detached perfection. The overall vibe is one of chilling elegance and emotional absence, embodying a perfect, cold escape from human connection.
