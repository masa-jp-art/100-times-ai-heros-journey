# ビジュアルプロンプト

## protagonist

Age: 22, lean muscular build, sharp jawline, determined eyes. Dark brown short hair, slightly tousled. Wears a tailored charcoal suit with subtle deep blue accents, a silver ring on left hand. Holds a weathered leather notebook. Atmosphere: confident, ambitious, poised. Lighting: dramatic spotlight from above, sharp shadows emphasizing his resolve.

## messenger

Age: ageless, ethereal form, translucent wind spirit. Slender, swirling silhouette with flowing silver hair. Wears a light azure robe that drifts like mist. Carries a luminous silver key. Atmosphere: mystical, airy, guiding. Lighting: soft diffused glow, gentle halo around him, highlighting ethereal presence.

## supporter

Age: 24, slender build, calm demeanor, kind eyes. Dark hair with silver streaks, tied in a loose knot. Wears a pastel violet dreamscape cloak with subtle star patterns. Holds a translucent dream map and a crystal orb. Atmosphere: supportive, warm, visionary. Lighting: warm golden light, soft focus, comforting glow.

## adversary

Age: 48, broad-shouldered, imposing. Dark slicked-back hair, sharp gray eyes. Wears a tailored black suit with crimson lapel. Holds a leather contract and a hidden dagger. Atmosphere: ominous, ruthless, manipulative. Lighting: harsh high-contrast, deep shadows, a single red spotlight highlighting his threat.
